# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and collaborative. Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or deliberately expose another person's private information. Treat security reports, dataset provenance, and incident material with care.

## Responsible participation

Give actionable feedback, explain disagreements without personal attacks, credit other people's work, and pause when a discussion becomes unproductive. Security-sensitive concerns should be handled through the private reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this standard. Decisions should be consistent, proportionate, and focused on protecting a welcoming technical community.
